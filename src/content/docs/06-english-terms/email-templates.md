## 模板 1：展会后 48 小时跟进（48h Exhibition Follow-up）

适用场景：展台交换名片或微信后 48 小时内发出，用专业介绍锁定下一步资料收集，不在邮件里口头锁死运价（动作节奏见 [展会获客 SOP](../05-sales-business/exhibition-sop/)）。

**Subject:** `Nice meeting you at [Exhibition Name] – Project Cargo / OOG & Breakbulk Support`

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Great speaking with you at [Exhibition Name] on [Date / Day].

As discussed, our team focuses on project cargo, out-of-gauge (OOG) special containers (OT/FR), and breakbulk shipments [including energy storage equipment / heavy machinery, if applicable] from [Origin Region, e.g. China] to [Target Market, e.g. Southeast Asia / Europe].

If you have any upcoming shipments or active inquiries, feel free to share your cargo dimensions, gross weight, and target destination port. Once received, we will review the routing options and get back to you with a preliminary routing proposal within [1-2] working days.

Looking forward to staying in touch.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
[Your Role]
```

## 模板 2：询价资料不全反问（Request for Missing Inquiry Details）

适用场景：客户只发来简单品名、航线或「大概多少钱」时，礼貌复述已知信息并用清单要齐缺失项（字段口径见 [询价反问清单](../05-sales-business/inquiry-checklist/)）。

**Subject:** `Re: [Client Inquiry / Route, e.g. Shenzhen to Jakarta] – Cargo Details Needed for Routing Check`

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for your inquiry from [POL / Origin] to [POD / Destination].

Based on your message, we note the shipment involves [known commodity / quantity / timeline]. To check feasible vessel options, equipment availability, and prepare an actionable routing and quotation structure, could you please help confirm the missing details below?

1. Commodity & HS Code (and UN number / DG class if hazardous or battery-related)
2. Number of pieces, and whether stackable or dismountable
3. Final packed dimensions per piece (L x W x H in cm/m) and gross weight per piece
4. Excel packing list and cargo photos / technical drawings (showing lifting points & COG if available)
5. Exact POL / POD (or factory / site address if door delivery is required) and Incoterms
6. Stowage preference: under deck or on deck acceptable
7. Inquiry type: firm shipment, bidding, or budgetary estimate

Once we have these details, we can verify the routing feasibility and revert with a clear proposal.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 模板 3：初步路径建议（Preliminary Routing Proposal）

适用场景：资料基本齐备后，按 [项目运输方案怎么讲](../03-project-cargo/solution-framing/) 的五步结构输出路径对比与风险边界，不锁死价格。

**Subject:** `Preliminary Routing Proposal: [Commodity] – [POL] to [POD]`

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Thank you for sharing the cargo details. Here is our preliminary routing proposal based on your current information:

1. Cargo Summary
- Commodity: [Commodity]
- Quantity & Weight: [X pieces], [Gross Weight per piece / Total]
- Packed Dimensions: [L x W x H]
- Route & Terms: [POL] to [POD], [Incoterms]

2. Proposed Routing Options
- Option A (Special Container, e.g. [40'FR / 40'OT]):
  Best suited when dimensions fit carrier OOG limits and liner frequency is preferred. Subject to equipment availability, port crane capacity, and stowage confirmation ([under deck / on deck]).
- Option B (Breakbulk / MPP Vessel):
  Recommended if dimensions exceed container limits, multiple oversized units ship together, or vessel schedule flexibility is acceptable. Subject to laycan window, loading/discharging terms, and port handling conditions.

3. Key Assumptions & Risk Boundaries
- This proposal is subject to final packed dimensions, carrier approval, space & equipment availability, and DG compliance if applicable.
- For OOG / breakbulk cargo, free storage time at port and crane capacity at POL, transshipment port, and POD must be verified before booking.

4. Next Steps
Please confirm your preferred option and share [remaining documents / target shipment window] by [Date], so we can proceed with formal carrier checks and itemized cost structure.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 模板 4：向船东 / 代理询 OOG 或件杂价（Carrier / Agent Rate & Space Inquiry）

适用场景：内部初步判断走特种柜或件杂后，向船东或海外代理发正式询价邮件（字段参考 [特种柜](../03-project-cargo/special-containers/) 与 [件杂货询盘与计费](../03-project-cargo/bbk-inquiry/)）。

**Subject:** `OOG / BBK Inquiry: [POL] to [POD] – [Commodity] / [Cargo Readiness Window]`

```text
Dear [Carrier / Agent Name],

Please assist to check space, equipment availability, and rate structure for the following shipment:

- POL / POD: [Port of Loading] / [Port of Discharge]
- Commodity & Packing: [Commodity], packed in [Packing Type]
- Quantity: [Number of Pieces]
- Packed Dimensions (per piece): [L x W x H cm]
- Gross Weight (per piece / total): [Weight kg/MT]
- COG & Lifting Points: [As per attached drawing / Marked / Not blocked]
- Requested Mode:
  [For OOG]: [e.g. 1 x 40'FR, panels up/down], OOG type [OW+OH / OL+OW+OH], stowage [under deck preferred / on deck acceptable]
  [For BBK]: Breakbulk / MPP, preferred terms [FLT / FILO / FIO / FIOST], laycan [Date Window], stackable [Yes / No]
- Additional Checks: Please confirm killed slots (if FR/OT), terminal lifting / handling charges, port crane capacity, and OOG free storage terms.

Attached please find the Excel packing list and cargo photos for your review.

Thanks and best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 模板 5：订舱前资料与红线确认（Pre-Booking Confirmation & Compliance Check）

适用场景：客户口头确认出货后、正式下订舱单前发出，用书面形式把尺寸实测、带电合规、VGM 与免堆差异一次说清（交接清单见 [订舱与执行交接清单](../05-sales-business/booking-handoff/)，带电红线见 [锂电池运输红线](../04-energy-storage/battery-dg-rules/)）。

**Subject:** `Pre-Booking Confirmation & Compliance Check: [Commodity] – [POL] to [POD]`

```text
Hi [Client Name],

Before we place the formal booking with the carrier, please confirm the following operational and compliance items in writing:

1. Final Packed Dimensions & Gross Weight
   Please confirm the final packed measurements ([L x W x H]) and gross weight, and ensure all lifting points / lashing points remain unobstructed after packing.
2. Lithium Battery / Dangerous Goods Compliance (if applicable)
   If the cargo contains lithium batteries or any regulated DG components, complete SDS (EN/CN), UN38.3 test summary/report, and required transport / DG packaging certificates must be provided prior to booking. We cannot place a booking or commit to a sailing date with incomplete DG documentation.
3. VGM Submission (for Containerized / OT / FR Cargo)
   Verified Gross Mass (VGM) must be submitted accurately before the carrier cut-off as a mandatory loading condition.
4. OOG Storage & Port Handling Terms
   Please note that OOG special containers may not enjoy standard container free storage days at port, and final stowage ([under deck / on deck]) remains subject to carrier approval.

Kindly reply to confirm the above points and attach any pending documents so we can proceed with booking.

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

## 复述

展会 48 小时跟进只承诺给路径建议，不在邮件里口头锁死价。  
向船东询 OOG 或件杂价时，要同时写清尺寸超限组合、舱内或甲板偏好与装卸口径。  
涉及带电或危险品时，订舱前必须书面核齐合规资料，不承诺先订舱后补件。

**复习题：** 客户只发来一句 “Need freight from Shenzhen to Jakarta next week, 1 battery cabinet”，应该先套用哪两个模板的逻辑？
